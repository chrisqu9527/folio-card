Name: [CHARACTER NAME]
Hair: [length, color, crown, bangs] — real hair intact, red rim on the right; illusion hair fractures into shards
Eyes: [iris color], sharp, seeing through the fake; no spiral unless asked
Build: [build], fair skin, clean cel planes on the real body only
Outfit: [exact outfit lock] — no new accessories
Expression: cold recognition, mouth set or barely open, not comedy, not a scream unless asked

LEFT / RIGHT LAW: Left third is the light and the real character stepping through. Center is the face and the breaking point. Right third is the dark field and the collapsing false self. Never flip this.

The real character, low angle, weight forward, hand or blade at the vertical center.
The hit: one hard-edged crimson fracture, illusion face split, shards as flat triangles.
The field: black void, red triangular panels, a peeling false shrine or halo collapsing on the right.

ENVIRONMENT: Abstract horror arena. A fake sacred space — gold ring, white veil, or altar — tearing into black and crimson geometry. No photo street, no realistic church interior.
PAINTING TREATMENT: Clean line, soft-cel only inside hard shadow shapes, antique metal with flat highlight, horror limited to the illusion and the shards. No bloom, no film grain, no watermark, no UI text.
