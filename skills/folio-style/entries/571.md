## 提示词

```
Create a photorealistic live-action zombie-horror sequence set inside a modern university biomedical research laboratory during an ordinary afternoon practical class. The environment should feel authentic and lived-in: long stainless-steel workstations, glass cabinets, microscopes, centrifuges, laptops, specimen trays, chemical bottles, safety posters, sinks, stools and fluorescent ceiling panels. Several college students are conducting routine experiments while a young adult Korean female student works at a station near the center of the room.

Keep the female protagonist's face, hairstyle, clothing, body proportions and overall identity perfectly consistent throughout the entire video. She wears a clean white laboratory coat over a simple dark shirt, blue jeans and white sneakers. Her appearance must remain realistic and unchanged until the infection begins.

The sequence starts calmly. Students discuss their experiments quietly while recording observations on laptops and laboratory notebooks. The camera moves naturally through the room with subtle handheld movement, occasionally passing behind students and laboratory equipment, creating the feeling of an actual person filming the situation.

A refrigerated specimen cabinet suddenly begins vibrating. A faint clicking sound comes from inside. One student approaches it and notices condensation spreading across the glass. Before anyone can react, the cabinet door pops open slightly and a sealed biological sample rolls onto the floor.

The container does not explode. Instead, a thin dark liquid slowly leaks from a hairline fracture and spreads across the laboratory floor. The protagonist notices it and steps backward. A small amount splashes onto the lower edge of her laboratory coat and crawls upward across the fabric as if reacting to body heat.

She tries wiping it away, but the substance suddenly contracts and grips her wrist.

She freezes.

Her breathing becomes rapid. The surrounding students initially think she has been injured and move toward her. She tells them to stay back. The dark material disappears beneath her skin.

A subtle transformation begins.

Small branching discolorations appear around her wrist and slowly travel toward her forearm. Her fingertips become rigid and begin trembling. She grips the edge of the laboratory bench for support as her breathing becomes heavier. Her face remains recognizable and realistic, but her complexion gradually loses its warmth.

One student reaches for her shoulder.

She suddenly jerks away with unexpected force.

The room falls silent.

The camera moves closer as she struggles to control her movements. Her pupils become increasingly dark and cloudy while faint black vascular patterns spread beneath the skin around her temples and jaw. She looks frightened for a moment, as though she is still aware of what is happening.

Then her expression goes completely blank.

A centrifuge suddenly falls from a nearby bench as she turns toward the sound.

She moves toward another student far too quickly.

The student retreats between two laboratory benches, knocking over stools and equipment. She follows with stiff, increasingly unnatural movements, striking the bench between them hard enough to send equipment sliding across the surface.

The other students finally realize something is seriously wrong.

One grabs the emergency phone while another starts pulling classmates toward the exit. A student accidentally knocks over a tray of glass tubes, sending them scattering across the floor.

The infected woman suddenly stops.

Completely motionless.

Everyone looks back at her.

She slowly raises her head.

Her eyes are now almost entirely black and cloudy. Dark veins are visible beneath the skin of her neck and cheeks. She tilts her head slightly, listening to the movement of the escaping students.

Then she runs.

The camera follows the survivors through the narrow laboratory aisles in frantic handheld shots. Students push past chairs and equipment while trying to reach the emergency exit. One student slips but is pulled back up by a classmate.

As they reach the door, another specimen cabinet in the background suddenly shatters.

A student who had been hiding beneath a workstation begins convulsing.

His body arches violently against the floor as dark vascular patterns spread across his neck. The surrounding students scream and rush through the doorway.

The laboratory emergency system activates.

The normal fluorescent lights shut off.

Red emergency illumination begins pulsing through the room.

The survivors manage to pull the heavy fire door closed. Someone wedges a metal chair beneath the handle while another student holds the door shut as something repeatedly strikes it from inside.

Silence.

The students stand in the hallway, breathing heavily and staring at the sealed laboratory.

Then the banging stops.

One survivor slowly approaches the narrow observation window.

At first, the laboratory appears empty.

Broken glass covers the floor. Chairs are overturned. Equipment lights blink in the darkness.

Then a shadow moves behind a workstation.

The infected female student slowly steps into the red emergency light.

She is still wearing the same white laboratory coat, jeans and sneakers. Her clothing is slightly disheveled but intact. Her pale face remains recognizable, with dark branching veins visible around her neck and temples.

She stands completely still.

The camera slowly pushes closer toward the glass.

Her black eyes suddenly shift toward the camera.

She takes one slow step forward.

Then another.

She stops directly in front of the observation window.

For several seconds she simply stares.

A faint, unnatural smile gradually appears on her face.

The hallway lights flicker.

CUT TO BLACK.
```
