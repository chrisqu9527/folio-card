## 提示词

```
Create a 9:16 vertical high fashion editorial poster.

TITLE: [TITLE]
SUBJECT: [SUBJECT]
CORE VISUAL DEVICE: [CORE VISUAL DEVICE]
ACCENT COLOR: [ACCENT COLOR]
MOOD: [MOOD]

Build the composition around the interaction between portrait photography and typography. The model should not simply stand beside the text. Make the typography physically participate in the image through overlap, masking, cropping, refraction, depth, foreground and background separation, or spatial interruption.

Use one strong visual mechanism per poster and make it immediately readable at thumbnail size.

Create a clear editorial hierarchy:

1. One oversized display title as the main graphic anchor
2. The fashion portrait as the primary photographic subject
3. A few meaningful editorial captions and credits
4. One controlled graphic or physical intervention

Use refined fashion typography, combining high contrast serif, condensed sans serif, modern grotesk, or subtle handwritten lettering. Large type can extend beyond the canvas and be partially hidden by the model. Small text should stay minimal, precise and meaningful rather than filling the page with random words.

Use an asymmetric editorial grid, bold cropping, generous negative space and strong scale contrast. Allow the model, type and graphic elements to move between foreground, middle ground and background.

Keep the palette restrained. Use mostly neutral tones such as ivory, soft gray, black or muted blue, with only one dominant accent color.

The portrait should feel like real fashion photography with natural skin texture, realistic fabric, believable hair and refined studio lighting. Avoid glossy CGI skin, excessive retouching and generic commercial modeling poses.

The final result should feel like an independent fashion magazine, experimental editorial campaign or art directed fashion poster, not a social media template.

Avoid meaningless filler text, excessive decoration, numbered labels, Vol or Issue numbering, sparkle symbols, random logos, cluttered layouts, cheap gradients and repetitive poster formulas.
```
