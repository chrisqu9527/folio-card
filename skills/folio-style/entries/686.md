```
2x2 grid, 16:9, do this for 4 days that changed science 
INPUT: $ SUBJECT

Style = {

  <semantic_typography>

  MEDIUM:
  High-contrast graphic typography
  combined with symbolic visual storytelling.

  LETTERFORMS:
  Heavy black geometric sans-serif letters,
  clever counters, altered terminals,
  object-shaped strokes, modular construction.

  SEMANTIC DESIGN:
  Every major graphic modification must
  reference the meaning of $ SUBJECT.

  TYPOGRAPHIC INTEGRATION:
  Relevant objects, inventions, concepts,
  tools, motions, or abstract symbols
  become working parts of the letters.

  VISUAL HIERARCHY:
  One dominant readable subject title.

  </semantic_typography>

  <impossible_isometric_structure>

  GEOMETRY:
  Isometric beams, interlocking polygons,
  Penrose-like structures, continuous ribbons,
  folded angular forms, perspective paradoxes.

  COLOR:
  Black, dark charcoal, medium gray,
  plus selective cyan, orange, magenta,
  green, or other inferred accents.

  DEPTH:
  Flat graphic dimensionality,
  not realistic photographic depth.

  SHADING:
  Simple planar faces.
  Crisp geometric boundaries.
  Minimal shadows.

  EFFECT:
  Geometrically fascinating.
  A visual puzzle hidden inside typography.

  </impossible_isometric_structure>

  <expressive_character_illustration>

  MEDIUM:
  Minimal black-and-white cartoon illustration.

  CHARACTER:
  An appealing subject-derived figure,
  object, symbol, or mascot.

  FACIAL LANGUAGE:
  Simple dot eyes, minimal mouth,
  exaggerated expression where appropriate.

  BODY LANGUAGE:
  Funny poses, simplified limbs,
  expressive silhouettes.

  LINEWORK:
  Thick ink outlines, occasional
  rough brush edges, deliberate imperfections.

  COLOR:
  Primarily black and white.

  </expressive_character_illustration>

  <humorous_editorial_lettering>

  TYPOGRAPHY:
  Loose hand lettering, informal brush script,
  large uneven display characters,
  playful changes in scale and rhythm.

  TEXT ENGINE:
  Infer a concise line or phrase
  appropriate to $ SUBJECT.

  TONE:
  Select from wit, irony, wonder,
  optimism, sarcasm, confidence,
  mischief, or thoughtful restraint.

  COMPOSITION:
  Lettering wraps around, interacts with,
  or becomes part of the illustration.

  </humorous_editorial_lettering>

  <transformation>

  STEP 1:
  Identify the subject's central meaning
  and most recognizable visual identity.

  STEP 2:
  Convert that identity into a short,
  bold typographic title.

  STEP 3:
  Replace selected letter components
  with meaningful symbolic geometry.

  STEP 4:
  Construct a clever impossible
  three-dimensional relationship
  inside or between the letterforms.

  STEP 5:
  Extract an expressive character
  or humorous visual metaphor
  from the subject's personality.

  STEP 6:
  Allow the character to interact
  directly with the typography.

  STEP 7:
  Introduce one short hand-lettered
  statement that amplifies the concept.

  STEP 8:
  Reconcile all elements into
  one coherent graphic composition.

  </transformation>

  <adaptive_subject_logic>

  IF SUBJECT IS TECHNOLOGICAL:
  Emphasize mechanical letter structures,
  geometric logic, clever interface symbols.

  IF SUBJECT IS CULTURAL:
  Emphasize meaningful identity symbols,
  expressive lettering, iconic silhouettes.

  IF SUBJECT IS HISTORICAL:
  Emphasize recognizable motifs,
  intelligent letter substitutions,
  period-appropriate personality.

  IF SUBJECT IS SCIENTIFIC:
  Emphasize geometric structures,
  dimensional contradictions,
  symbolic mechanisms.

  IF SUBJECT IS EMOTIONAL:
  Emphasize the expressive character,
  humorous visual metaphor,
  and hand-lettered message.

  IF SUBJECT IS ABSTRACT:
  Invent a visual metaphor,
  then convert that metaphor
  into a readable typographic structure.

  </adaptive_subject_logic>

  <finish>

  Beautifully clean graphic surfaces.

  Predominantly black on warm white.

  Strong lettering with controlled
  accent colors.

  One immediately understandable
  visual idea.

  Clever hidden details.

  Expressive cartoon personality.

  Impressive impossible geometry.

  Premium illustration and branding quality.

  </finish>

  <avoid>

  Avoid literal replication of reference subjects.

  Avoid default cats, coffee cups,
  inventors, or science illustrations.

  Avoid optical illusions that
  destroy word readability.

  Avoid overly detailed mascots.

  Avoid random decorative symbols.

  Avoid photorealistic rendering.

  Avoid generic corporate templates.

  Avoid disconnected visual elements.

  </avoid>

}

OUTPUT:

A sophisticated and entertaining
typographic illustration where $ SUBJECT
becomes a bold graphic identity,
a playful visual joke, and an
impossible geometric construction.

The artwork should feel deceptively simple,
visually intelligent, and full of personality.
```
